## 1. The atomic guarded update: the flat reservation race (TOCTOU)

Alice and Bob both prove the guard `forall x in S. NotRevoked(x)` against the same registry state.

| Profile | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Naive (no policy bound, the landlord checks the guard, then revokes) | double booking: 2 members of S revoked |
| CAS (policy bound to Revoke, group CAS) | second guarded revocation rejected on chain: `StaleGroupVersion` |
| Loser's proof `!(forall x in S. NotRevoked(x))` → `exists x in S. Revoked(x)` (stable) | valid: true; still valid after later issuance and revocation: true |
| Alice: `exists x in S. Sig(x, "Landlord") & Revoked(x)` (hidden member) | valid: true |
